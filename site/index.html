Better Canvas adds AI assistance, mind maps, and six themes to Obsidian's built-in Canvas. Install and enable the plugin, then use these features directly on your canvas in Obsidian.

AI assistant

Keep the answer
next to the question.

Select one card or a group in Obsidian Canvas and ask AI with your existing context. Answers become new nodes connected to their sources, so every follow-up has somewhere to start.

Start with what you know

Summarize sources, compare viewpoints, and expand an idea. Watch answers stream in, stop, or regenerate.

Give understanding a structure

Generate a mind map or learning flashcards. Export a Markdown deck for Spaced Repetition.

Choose your own model

Use OpenAI-compatible APIs, Anthropic, or Gemini. Connect installed, signed-in local agents such as Codex and OpenCode.

AI setup guide

AI is optional and needs your configuration. Services may charge for usage. Prompts and related context go to your chosen service; local agents may also call remote models. Editing, mapping, and themes work without AI.

Mind maps

A new thought.
Another branch.

Build mind maps on Obsidian's native canvas. Create children and siblings from the keyboard, arrange branches in four directions, or start a map from the H1 headings in a Markdown note.

While editing text, use Shift + Tab / Shift + Enter to create nodes. Shortcut groups can be disabled in settings.

Canvas themes

Same ideas.
A different expression.

Give each Obsidian canvas its own theme. Loose ideas, reading notes, process maps, or sketches: choose a look that fits while keeping your text, positions, and connections.

Real plugin screenshots. Theme choices save with the canvas; node styles can be applied to several selected cards at once.
